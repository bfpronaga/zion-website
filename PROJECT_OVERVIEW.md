## Hosting & Platform Options

### Option 1: Traditional CMS (WordPress)
**Pros:**
- Familiar to many users
- Extensive plugin ecosystem
- Easy content management
- SEO-friendly out of the box
- Many hosting options
- Newsletter plugins available

**Cons:**
- Requires regular maintenance and updates
- Can become slow with many plugins
- Security concerns if not properly maintained

**Hosting Recommendations:**
- Kinsta (Premium, managed WordPress)
- SiteGround (Good balance of features/price)
- WP Engine (Enterprise-grade)
- Xserver (Japanese hosting, good support)

**Estimated Monthly Cost:** ¥1,000 - ¥5,000

---

### Option 2: 日本製 Headless CMS (microCMS / Newt + Next.js) ⭐ 推奨
**Pros:**
- Modern, fast performance
- Complete control over design
- API-first approach
- Scalable
- Great developer experience
- Future-proof

**Cons:**
- Requires technical knowledge for setup
- Higher initial development cost
- More complex for non-technical editors initially

**Hosting Recommendations:**
- Vercel (for Next.js frontend)
- Railway/Render (for Strapi backend)
- AWS/Azure (for full control)

**Estimated Monthly Cost:** ¥2,000 - ¥8,000

---

### Option 3: All-in-One Platform (Webflow)
**Pros:**
- Visual editor (no coding needed)
- Beautiful templates
- CMS built-in
- Fast performance
- Hosting included
- Great for designers

**Cons:**
- Can be expensive for enterprise features
- Less flexibility than custom solution
- Learning curve for the editor

**Estimated Monthly Cost:** $29 - $99 USD (¥4,000 - ¥14,000)

---

### Option 4: Japanese-Focused CMS (a-blog cms / MovableType)
**Pros:**
- Japanese language support
- Good for Japanese organizations
- Local support available
- Japanese hosting integration

**Cons:**
- Less international community
- Fewer plugins/themes
- May be outdated compared to modern solutions

**Estimated Monthly Cost:** ¥3,000 - ¥10,000

---

### Option 5: Notion + Super.so (Quick & Easy)
**Pros:**
- Extremely easy to use
- Notion's familiar interface
- No technical knowledge required
- Fast deployment
- Very affordable

**Cons:**
- Limited customization
- Less control over design
- Not ideal for large-scale sites
- No traditional CMS features

**Estimated Monthly Cost:** $12 - $36 USD (¥1,700 - ¥5,000)

## Recommended Solution

### **Primary Recommendation: WordPress + Modern Theme**

**Reasoning:**
1. **User-Friendly:** Staff can easily manage content without technical knowledge
2. **Cost-Effective:** Good balance of features and affordability
3. **Newsletter Integration:** Excellent email marketing plugins (Mailchimp, SendinBlue)
4. **Video Management:** Easy YouTube integration and video galleries
5. **Japanese Support:** Wide availability of Japanese themes and plugins
6. **Maintenance:** Easy to find support and developers in Japan

**Recommended Setup:**
- **Hosting:** Xserver (Japanese hosting with good support)
- **Theme:** Modern, responsive Japanese-friendly theme (e.g., Lightning, Swell)
- **Plugins:**
  - Newsletter: MailPoet or Newsletter
  - Video Gallery: Youtube Channel Gallery
  - Events: The Events Calendar
  - SEO: Yoast SEO
  - Security: Wordfence
  - Backup: UpdraftPlus
  - Forms: Contact Form 7 or WPForms

**Total Setup Cost Estimate:** ¥200,000 - ¥500,000 (one-time)
**Monthly Operating Cost:** ¥2,000 - ¥4,000

---

### **Alternative Recommendation: Custom Next.js + Headless CMS**

**For Better Performance & Modern Experience:**

**推奨: microCMS** 🥇

**Tech Stack:**
- **Frontend:** Next.js 14 with App Router
- **CMS:** microCMS (日本製、日本語対応)
- **Hosting:** Vercel (frontend)
- **Email:** SendGrid or AWS SES
- **Video:** YouTube API integration
- **Image:** microCMS Image Optimization API

**Benefits:**
- ✅ 日本語管理画面・ドキュメント完備
- ✅ 非技術者でも使いやすい
- ✅ 強力な画像最適化API
- ✅ Lightning-fast performance
- ✅ 導入実績7,000社以上（安心感）
- ✅ 充実したコミュニティ・サポート
- ✅ Next.js公式SDK

**Alternative: Newt** 🥈
- ✅ より低コスト（月¥2,000〜）
- ✅ GraphQL対応
- ✅ 高度なワークフロー機能
- ✅ App拡張で柔軟にカスタマイズ可能

**詳細比較:** [`docs/CMS_COMPARISON.md`](docs/CMS_COMPARISON.md) 参照

**Total Setup Cost Estimate:** ¥300,000 - ¥800,000 (one-time)
